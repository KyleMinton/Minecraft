Sounds
======

Minecraft Beta 1.8's own sounds (from .minecraft/resources/newsound and
streaming), played by the names the game uses. A name's dots are folders:

    step.stone        step/stone1.ogg .. stone4.ogg (a random one each time)
    random.click      random/click.ogg
    tile.piston.out   tile/piston/out.ogg
    ambient.cave.cave ambient/cave/cave1.ogg .. cave13.ogg

Folders:

    step/      footsteps, and (as in Beta) placing and breaking blocks
    random/    clicks, pops, doors, explosions, fizz, fuse, glass, hurt, bow...
    liquid/    water ambience and splashes
    fire/      fire crackle and flint and steel
    note/      note block instruments (harp, bd, snare, hat, bassattack)
    portal/    portal ambience
    tile/      piston/in.ogg and piston/out.ogg
    ambient/   cave sounds in dark places
    records/   13.ogg and cat.ogg for the jukebox (streamed)

Beta has no separate dig sounds: breaking a block plays its step sound
(glass uses random.glass, sand uses step.gravel).
